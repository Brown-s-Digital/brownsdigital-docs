The first time you sign in, Studio walks you through a short setup. You can leave and come back. Studio remembers your place.

*Last checked: 21 November 2026.*

## Sign in

1. Open [https://studio.brownsdigital.org/](https://studio.brownsdigital.org/).
2. On the "Welcome back" screen, choose a sign-in option: **Continue with passkey**, the **Google** button, the **GitHub** button, or scan the code with your phone.

If the code is hard to scan, use **Try a simpler code**.

Studio sign-in does not use a password. There is no email sign-up.

The account page is [https://brownsdigital.org/auth](https://brownsdigital.org/auth).

The public sign-in screen includes this line: "By continuing, you agree to our [Terms of service](https://brownsdigital.org/terms/) and [Privacy policy](https://brownsdigital.org/privacy/)."

## Step 1 of 3: Our policies

This step is labelled Step 1 of 3 and titled Our policies. It shows the policies that apply when you use Studio, including the [Terms of Service](https://brownsdigital.org/policies/terms/), the [Privacy Policy](https://brownsdigital.org/policies/privacy/), and the [Acceptable Use Policy](https://brownsdigital.org/policies/acceptable-use/), with links to read them in full.

1. Read the policies on the screen.
2. Tick the box to confirm that you agree to them. The label on the box is: "I have read and agree to these policies for my account and published sites."
3. Choose **Agree and continue**. If you have already agreed, the button says **Continue**.

You need to finish this step before you can go further.

Users must be 13 or older. Users aged 13 to 17 may use Studio only with a parent or guardian's consent, as set out in the [Terms of Service](https://brownsdigital.org/policies/terms/).

## Set up your profile

The next sheet is titled **Set up your profile**.

1. Optionally add a profile photo. Studio accepts JPEG, PNG, WebP, or GIF files.
2. Enter a display name if you want one (up to 64 characters).
3. Enter a username. It appears with an @ in front.
4. Choose **Next**.

A username must be at least 3 characters and at most 32. Use letters, numbers, and underscores. Studio removes other characters.

The Next button stays unavailable until the username is valid.

## Create a site next

After your profile is saved, Studio offers to create a site. You can create one now, or look around Console first and come back later. [Create a site](/get-started/create-a-site) covers that flow.

## After you are in Studio

The left sidebar includes **Console**, **Sites**, and **Settings**. It also links to Docs, Help, and Status.

From the account menu you can open account settings, change appearance, turn **Simple mode** on or off, lock Studio, or log out.

Simple mode hides some advanced tools, including Health, Crew, and Agent. It does not change the text size. You can turn it off again from the same menu.

If you choose **Lock screen**, Studio later asks for a 4-digit PIN to unlock. If you forget that PIN, use **Contact support** on the unlock screen, which opens a Help ticket.

## If something goes wrong

- If sign-in fails, try another option on the same screen, or email [info@brownsdigital.org](mailto:info@brownsdigital.org).
- If a username is too short, Studio says: "Usernames need at least 3 characters."
- If a username is too long, Studio says: "Usernames can be at most 32 characters."
- If the profile will not save, check the username and try again.
- If the screen says "Studio is paused", wait and try again shortly.
- If Studio asks for a PIN and you have forgotten it, open a ticket from the unlock screen or email [info@brownsdigital.org](mailto:info@brownsdigital.org).
