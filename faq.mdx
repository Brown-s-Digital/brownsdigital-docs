Short answers about plans, credits, publishing, domains, and support.

<Warning>
  Coming soon until Studio and main deploy. Answers below that mention `{slug}.app.brownsdigital.org` hosts or custom domains on Build and Forge describe planned product behaviour. Use what Studio and Account show for your plan today.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What plans are available?">
    Studio plans are **Draft** (free), **Build**, **Forge**, and **Architect**.

    - **Draft** — 1 site
    - **Build** — 1 site, custom CSS, custom domain, 250 credits per month
    - **Forge** — 3 sites, custom CSS, custom domains, 3 collaborators, 750 credits per month
    - **Architect** — unlimited sites, custom CSS, custom domains, unlimited collaborators, 2,500 credits per month

    Every plan includes an `app.brownsdigital.org` address (`{slug}.app.brownsdigital.org`). Current prices and checkout are on [pricing](https://brownsdigital.org/#pricing). Paid checkout is in South African rand (ZAR).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are credits?">
    Credits are a monthly allotment on paid plans. Studio uses them for billable actions when a cost applies. Defaults can be zero for some actions, so the product does not always charge credits for every click.

    Your balance and top-ups live in [Account](https://brownsdigital.org/my/) when those controls are available for your plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I preview and publish a site?">
    Open the **Publish** modal. Use **Preview** for a private preview, then **Live** to push publicly. Preview URLs look like `https://{slug}.app.brownsdigital.org/staging/`. Live sites use `https://{slug}.app.brownsdigital.org/` (or the hostname Studio shows).

    Your web address (slug) locks after the first publish. Full guide: [Preview and publish](/studio/publishing). Step-by-step: [Create a Site](/welcome/create-a-site). New accounts: [Account Onboarding](/welcome/onboarding).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use a custom domain?">
    Custom domains are on **Build**, **Forge**, and **Architect** (including the older studio plan alias). Connect the domain in Studio or Account when your plan allows it; SSL follows the product domain flow. Draft keeps the included `app.brownsdigital.org` address only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I manage or cancel billing?">
    Open [Account](https://brownsdigital.org/my/) for subscription and billing. Cancel or change plan there when the controls are shown for your account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get help?">
    Email [info@brownsdigital.org](mailto:info@brownsdigital.org). For billing or profile questions, you can also use [Account](https://brownsdigital.org/my/).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
