Custom domains are on **Build**, **Forge**, and **Architect** (including the older studio plan alias). Draft keeps the included `app.brownsdigital.org` address only.

1. Confirm your plan allows custom domains ([Plans](/studio/plans)).
2. In Studio or [Account](https://brownsdigital.org/my/), start connecting the domain and follow the DNS records shown.
3. At your registrar, add the records Studio asks for. Keep existing mail (MX) records unless you intend to change email for that domain — domain attach for the site is separate from mail.
4. After DNS verifies, SSL is issued through the product attach flow. Studio shows when the domain is connected.

Domain actions use the same credit rules as other billable actions (default **0**, Staff override possible). See [Credits](/studio/credits).
