Credits are a monthly allotment on paid plans. Studio checks and spends them only when an action has a non-zero cost.

## Default action costs

Today the product defaults are:

- Preview (Publish modal → Preview) — **0**
- Live (Publish modal → Push to Live / Update Live) — **0**
- Domain — **0**

With those defaults, preview and live push do not spend credits.

## Staff overrides

Staff can set **cost overrides** on an account for those actions to **0**, **1**, **2**, **3**, or **5** credits. When an override is greater than zero, Studio checks the balance and spends credits on that action (preview, live, or domain).

Do not assume a fixed price from marketing copy — the live cost is the default or the override on your account.

## Monthly plan credits

| Plan | Monthly credits |
| --- | ---: |
| Draft (free) | 0 |
| Build | 250 |
| Forge | 750 |
| Architect | 2,500 |

Draft includes **0** monthly credits. With default action costs of 0, you can still preview and publish on Draft until an override or a future default change adds a cost.

Balance, top-ups, and billing live in [Account](https://brownsdigital.org/my/) when those controls are available. Plan comparison: [Plans](/studio/plans). Short answers: [FAQ](/faq).
