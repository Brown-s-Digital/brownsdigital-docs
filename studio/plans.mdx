Studio plans and what each includes.

| Plan | Sites | Custom CSS | Collaborators | Custom domain | Credits / month |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Draft (free) | 1 | No | 0 | No | 0 |
| Build | 1 | Yes | 0 | Yes | 250 |
| Forge | 3 | Yes | 3 | Yes | 750 |
| Architect | Unlimited | Yes | Unlimited | Yes | 2,500 |

Every plan gets an `app.brownsdigital.org` address (`{slug}.app.brownsdigital.org`). Prices and checkout are on [pricing](https://brownsdigital.org/#pricing) (ZAR). Manage subscription in [Account](https://brownsdigital.org/my/).

Credits behaviour (defaults and overrides): [Credits](/studio/credits). Domains: [Custom domains](/studio/custom-domains).
