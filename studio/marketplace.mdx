A **block and template marketplace** is on the longer-term [roadmap](https://brownsdigital.org/roadmap/). It is not shipping yet.

## What exists today

- Built-in **site templates** when you create a site (including Pitch, Lustre, Workshop, and the product/creative packs).
- The visual builder **block library** (Conversion, Graphic, and the rest).
- Staff already tracks marketplace-style **add-ons** and feedback for future product work.

## What the marketplace is for

When it ships, the intent is:

1. Browse curated **blocks** and **site templates** beyond the defaults.
2. Install a pack into your Studio site without rebuilding from a blank canvas.
3. Start with **first-party** Browns Digital packs; community submissions come later if quality and review hold up.

Payments, ratings, and third-party publishers are out of scope until the first-party catalogue is solid.

## Until then

Use the template picker and block library, watch the [changelog](https://brownsdigital.org/changelog/) for new built-in packs, and suggest ideas on [Discord](https://discord.gg/browns).
