Open Studio, create your account, and finish the first-run setup so you can create a site.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Studio">
    Go to [studio.brownsdigital.org](https://studio.brownsdigital.org/). You can also start from [brownsdigital.org](https://brownsdigital.org/) or open [Account](https://brownsdigital.org/my/) later for profile and subscription.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Choose **Create account** and sign up with email and password. On the sign-in screen you can also use Google, GitHub, or a passkey when those options are shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Studio emails a verification link or code. Confirm it, then sign in. Until that succeeds, you stay on the verify screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="First-run Studio setup">
    After sign-in, Studio walks you through welcome, Terms of Service, and profile basics (display name and username). Next comes site setup — template, details, and look — covered in [Create a Site](/welcome/create-a-site).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Account later">
    Subscription, profile, and privacy live at [brownsdigital.org/my](https://brownsdigital.org/my/). You can open Account from Studio any time after onboarding.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Some environments are invite-only (private QA). If sign-up is closed, use the invite you were given or contact support.
</Tip>

Next: [Create a Site](/welcome/create-a-site).
