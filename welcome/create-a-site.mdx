Create a site in Studio, preview it on staging, then promote it to live.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a site">
    Finish [Account Onboarding](/welcome/onboarding), or open **Sites** and create another site. New sites reuse the same wizard: template → details → look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    Pick a template or a blank canvas. You can change pages and blocks later in the builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and web address">
    Set the site name and **web address** (slug). That address becomes part of your staging and live URLs. It locks after the first publish, so choose carefully.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build in the canvas">
    Open the site in the visual builder. Add pages and blocks, adjust look, and save as you go. For page structure and navigation, see [Navigation](/builder/structure/navigation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview on staging">
    From Publish or the console, publish to **staging**. Open the staging URL Studio shows — typically `https://studio.brownsdigital.org/s/{slug}/staging/`. Staging is a private preview, not your live site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Promote to live">
    When the preview looks right, **promote** (publish from staging to production). Live sites use `{slug}.brownsdigital.app` (or the hostname Studio shows). The previous live version stays until promote succeeds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Custom domains are available on plans that allow them. Connect a domain later from Studio or Account when your plan supports it. See [pricing](https://brownsdigital.org/#pricing).
</Info>
