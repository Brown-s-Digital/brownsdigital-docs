Create a site in Studio, preview it, then push it live from the Publish modal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a site">
    Finish [Account Onboarding](/welcome/onboarding), or open **Sites** and create another site. New sites reuse the same wizard: template → details → look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    Pick a template or a blank canvas. You can change pages and blocks later in the builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and web address">
    Set the site name and **web address** (slug). That address becomes part of your preview and live URLs. It locks after the first publish, so choose carefully.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build in the canvas">
    Open the site in the visual builder. Add pages and blocks, adjust look, and save as you go. For page structure and navigation, see [Navigation](/builder/structure/navigation). Conversion blocks such as [API action](/builder/conversion/api-action) live under Builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    Open **Publish**, choose the **Preview** tab, and create or update the preview. Open the URL Studio shows — typically `https://{slug}.app.brownsdigital.org/staging/`. Preview is not your live site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Push to live">
    When the preview looks right, switch to **Live** and **Push to Live**. Live sites use `https://{slug}.app.brownsdigital.org/` (or the hostname Studio shows). The previous live version stays until the push succeeds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Custom domains are on Build, Forge, and Architect. Connect a domain later from Studio or Account. See [pricing](https://brownsdigital.org/#pricing). SEO titles and descriptions can be set in the builder; publish also auto-fills gaps from page content — [Preview and publish](/studio/publishing).
</Info>
