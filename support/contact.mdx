*Last checked: 20 November 2026.*

## Help centre

Start at the [Help centre](https://help.brownsdigital.org/). You can browse short answers there, or [open a ticket](https://help.brownsdigital.org/ticket/).

The ticket form asks for your name, email, a topic, a subject, and a short message. After you submit, the page shows a ticket ID. Keep that ID.

## Email

The public contact address is [info@brownsdigital.org](mailto:info@brownsdigital.org).

## System status

To check whether a service is having trouble right now, visit [https://status.brownsdigital.org/](https://status.brownsdigital.org/).
